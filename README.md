## About SwipeLock

Hand someone your phone to show them one photo, without handing them your whole gallery.
SwipeLock pins the screen to a single item; unlocking takes your fingerprint or face.

**Key Features:**
- 🔒 Screen pinning released only by biometric authentication
- 🖼️ High-quality photo and video viewing
- 🛡️ Your photos never leave your device — no uploads, no copies, no scanning
- 🆓 Free, with no purchases, no premium tier and no usage limits
- 📱 Modern, clean interface

**Download on Google Play:** [Coming Soon]

> **Note on privacy:** your photos and videos stay on your device. The app does send a small
> amount of technical data to Firebase — an anonymous account identifier, device model and
> OS details, install date, crash reports and basic analytics. The
> [privacy policy](https://team-green-emerald.github.io/swipelock-privacy/privacy_policy.html)
